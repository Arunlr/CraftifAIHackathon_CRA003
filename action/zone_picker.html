Click to add points. Zones need 3+ points (finish with Enter or double-click). Lines and arrows finish automatically after 2 points. For lines, the label L→R means crossing from the left side to the right side of the arrow (as used by direction_names).
YAML for your config
  1. Set stream: width/height to the [streammux] width/height in the config PipeGen generated (often the same as your video resolution shown above).
  2. Zones are tested with each object's bottom-centre (feet / wheels). Use anchor: center for top-down views.
  3. Coordinates are 0..1, so they work at any resolution.
